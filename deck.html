Introducing

Wmaps

Route Explorer

A driving companion that discovers hidden stories along your route

The Problem

"You drive past a thousand interesting places every year and never know they're there."
  • Navigation apps get you from A to B — but ignore everything in between
  • Guidebooks focus on destinations, not the journey
  • The best stories are 300 metres off the highway, unmarked and unknown
  • Researching a road trip route takes hours of Googling

The Solution

Wmaps layers a discovery experience on top of any driving route.


Like having a knowledgeable passenger who says:

"Hey, in 200 metres on your left — there's a lighthouse built by Portuguese sailors in 1823 who never made it home."

Then shuts up until the next interesting thing.


Hands-free. Voice-first. Zero distraction.

How It Works

1
Enter your route — origin and destination with Google Maps autocomplete
2
Pick an explore mode — History, Dark & Weird, Pop Culture, Nature, or Roadside Americana
3
AI discovers hidden gems — scans 5km radius along your route, finds unique places, and generates story hooks
4
Start driving — as you approach a place, it announces it hands-free with voice + a cinematic card
5
"Tell me more" — one tap for a 3-sentence story narrated by AI in the mode's tone

5 Explore Modes

🏛
History — Battlefields, memorials, heritage sites. Tone: Ken Burns documentary
👻
Dark & Weird — Haunted places, abandoned sites, mysteries. Tone: Campfire story
🎬
Pop Culture — Filming locations, murals, arts districts. Tone: Excited movie fan
🌿
Nature — Waterfalls, trails, scenic overlooks, caves. Tone: Nature documentary
🍔
Roadside Americana — World's largest things, vintage diners, quirky stops. Tone: Road trip blogger

Tech Stack

Single HTML file. No backend. No build step. Pure frontend.

Maps & Discovery

Google Maps JS API Directions API Places API

Route drawing, POI discovery via keyword + type search along polyline sample points

AI Layer

Claude claude-sonnet-4-6 Anthropic API

Generates story hooks for every POI. "Tell me more" delivers 3-sentence narratives in mode-specific tones

Real-Time Driving

Geolocation API DeviceOrientation

GPS tracking with heading detection, compass integration, left/right POI positioning

Voice & UI

Web Speech API Tailwind CSS

Hands-free audio announcements, spring animations, cinematic POI cards

Under the Hood

📍
Polyline Sampling — decode route into coordinates, sample every ~15 miles, search 5km radius at each point
🔍
Smart Filtering — type + keyword searches, deduplicate by place ID, remove within 500m, score by rating × log(reviews) with hidden gem boost
🧠
AI Enrichment — single Claude API call generates story hooks for all POIs at once: "Where Confederate gold vanished in 1865"
🔔
Proximity Trigger — watchPosition fires within 300m, 2-min cooldown, bearing math for left/right detection, auto-skips passed POIs

Key Features

🎯 Turn-by-turn nav
Real directions with maneuver icons, distance to next turn, ETA

🗣️ Hands-free voice
Auto-announces places while driving — no screen interaction needed

🎬 Cinematic cards
Spring-animated POI cards with photos, ratings, and AI-written hooks

🧭 Compass tracking
Arrow follows your heading via GPS, device compass, or movement

📖 On-demand stories
"Tell me more" for a 3-sentence AI story in the mode's tone

🔇 Respectful UX
2-min cooldown, never repeats, auto-dismiss — no spam, no distraction
